Retrospective forecast review: damped SEIR vs the flatline baseline

Model E0_damp050 · 13 weekly forecast dates, 2026-05-09 to 2026-08-01 · 1–4 week horizons · scored against the 2026-11-01 release (weeks through 2026-08-29, at least 60 days old) · country, 5 provinces, 117 health zones

How the model we plan to submit would have done had it run every week from mid-May to early August, scored by the weighted interval score (WIS) against the hub's flatline baseline. These are finalized-data backtests: every fit saw complete counts, so reporting delays play no part here.

Key findings

  • The model beats the flatline at every level. Relative WIS is 0.71 for the country, 0.78 for provinces and 0.93 for zones (below 1 is better).
  • Its intervals are better calibrated. 90% coverage is 0.85, 0.89 and 0.93 against the flatline's 0.64, 0.61 and 0.88.
  • All of the advantage comes from the growth phase. For forecast dates up to 06-13, relative WIS is 0.58 (country) and 0.62 (provinces). From 06-20 onward, during the plateau, the model and the flatline are tied (1.00 and 0.99).
  • The gain grows with horizon at the country and province levels, from 0.93 at 1 week to 0.65–0.73 at 3–4 weeks. At the zone level it holds at about 0.93 for every horizon.
  • 47 of the 59 zones with cases beat the flatline. The 12 that don't are only slightly worse (up to 1.21, apart from two zones with 1 and 5 cases), mostly Ituri zones with sharp turns.
  • A known weakness: damping makes the 4-week forecasts lag sharp takeoffs (late May, Haut-Uele in July). The epidemic is now declining, a phase these backtests never covered.

2. By horizon

Relative WIS for 1- to 4-week-ahead forecasts, over all forecast dates.

What it shows: 1 week ahead, the model is only slightly better than the flatline (0.92–0.93 at every level). For the country and provinces the advantage widens to 0.62–0.73 at 3–4 weeks. Zone-level relative WIS stays near 0.93.

What it means: the flatline gets steadily worse with horizon whenever cases are trending, while the damped model follows the trend for a while and then levels off. That matters most for aggregates, where trends are clearest.

Damping R toward 1 slows the forecast decline, so expect some overprediction there.

What it shows:

  • Early forecasts underpredict the takeoff. The first forecast (05-09) stayed near 25 cases a week while the country rose past 300. From 05-16 the forecasts follow the rise but level off, which was correct for the plateau that followed.
  • Plateau forecasts track the counts in Ituri and the country.
  • Nord-Kivu's steady climb is followed with a lag: each forecast flattens out below where the counts went next.
  • Haut-Uele's July takeoff was missed by forecasts made before it and followed afterwards.
  • Tshopo and Bas-Uele have so few cases that both models forecast near zero.

What it means: every province beats the flatline. The consistent weakness is underpredicting sustained growth. That's the cost of damping R toward 1, and it is the trade-off that avoids the large overshoots of the undamped model.

5. Health zones

Relative WIS for every zone with at least one case, against its total cases. Zones with no cases (58 of 117) aren't shown: there both models forecast zero, the flatline's WIS is 0, and relative WIS is undefined. The model scores near 0 there too (mean WIS under 0.1).

What it shows: 47 of the 59 zones with cases beat the flatline (median relative WIS 0.86). The 12 that are worse are mostly slightly worse:

  • Ituri and other zones with sharp turns (Rwampara 1.07, Nyankunde 1.14, Fataki 1.21), where the counts reversed faster than the damped trend.
  • Two sporadic zones: Kayna (1 case, 1.50) and Gethy (5 cases, 1.38), where any spread around zero scores worse than the flatline's exact zero.

What it means: the model's gains at the province level aren't driven by a few zones. Most zones improve modestly. Where it loses, the cause is quick reversals in individual zones that the model smooths over.

6. Before submitting

These scores come from finalized data in a growth and plateau phase. Three things separate them from a live submission:

  • The current phase is untested. Ituri has fallen from about 400 cases a week in August to about 200 in late October. Damping toward R = 1 slows the forecast decline, so the model will probably overpredict, and the flatline may be hard to beat. Forecast dates after 08-01 can't be scored under the 60-day rule yet.
  • Reporting delays. Live fits see incomplete recent weeks; the 10-31 week shows only 150 cases so far. The reporting model's choice (v1 against v3–v5) is still open, and the damping value hasn't been tested on incomplete data, where it would probably need to be lower than 0.5.
  • An end-to-end run. The damped model hasn't yet been run live with the reporting model. The live fit covers about 30 weeks, against at most 18 here, so fit time and sampler diagnostics need checking.

Methods

  • Model (E0_damp050): deterministic daily SEIR per health zone with gravity mixing. Province log R is a weekly random walk; each zone's deviation from its province is a B-spline. In forecast weeks, each zone's log R is multiplied by 0.5 per week, so R decays halfway toward 1 each week. Fitted in Stan with 4 chains; the forecast uses 400 posterior samples.
  • Backtest: one fit per forecast date, using only weeks up to that date, from the finalized counts. 1–4 week forecasts for 117 zones; province and country forecasts are sums of zone samples.
  • Truth: the 2026-11-01 release, using only weeks at least 60 days old (through 2026-08-29).
  • Flatline baseline: the hubverse quantile baseline. Its median is the last observed count; its spread comes from the symmetrized week-to-week changes over the series, summed over the horizon and truncated at 0.
  • Scoring: WIS on the hub's 23 quantile levels, averaged over forecast dates, horizons and locations. Relative WIS is the ratio of mean WIS (model / flatline) over the same forecasts. Coverage is the share of truths inside the central 50% and 90% intervals.
  • Code: scripts/backtest_final.R (fits), scripts/score_final.R (scores), scripts/plot_forecasts_final.R (figures), scripts/build_zone_report.py forecast_review (this page).
